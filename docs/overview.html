THE SELECTED STAGE

Arrow keys move between stages.

Bring real observations

Upload a date/price CSV or explicitly fetch available source data. The built-in catalog supplies product names, not synthetic prices.

Build causal features

Choose frequency, lags, windows and detrending. Missing dates carry the last observation forward; unfinished weeks or months are excluded.

Compare chronologically

Train supported models and inspect validation results. A separate walk-forward backtest evaluates recursive future periods.

Forecast and export

Refit the selected estimator on available history and export forecasts with matching metadata. Cached fallback data remains available when refresh fails.

BEFORE YOU BEGIN

The questions worth asking.

Clear expectations make a better first session—and a better presentation.

Can I use it without live market access?

Yes. CSV upload provides an offline starting point. Live discovery and fetching are separate actions.

Are the forecast ranges guaranteed?

No. Residual-based ranges and hybrid blending are heuristic. Repeated model selection can overfit the validation choices.

Why is the newest week missing?

Only completed periods should represent a full weekly or monthly observation. The date alignment keeps training and forecasting on the same calendar.

Live prices are unavailable. How can I continue?

Upload a date/price CSV or inspect real cached history. Refreshing the catalog only supplies product names. Check source dates: cached or forward-filled observations can be stale and are not newly fetched prices.
